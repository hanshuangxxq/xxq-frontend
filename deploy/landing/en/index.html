XXQ Academic Affairs Management System

An all-in-one teaching management platform for universities — from course selection, scheduling and curricula to grades, exams, graduation projects and practical education, with students, faculty, departments and academic affairs working together in one place.

Feature Overview

Teaching Operations

Enrollment campaigns with course selection and withdrawal, smart class scheduling and teaching assignments, curriculum planning, and course administration — the entire teaching operation online.

Grades & Exams

Grade entry and statistical analysis, exam and make-up exam scheduling, and student grade review requests — fully traceable grade data.

Practical Education

Unified management of internships, competitions and social practice; graduation projects cover the full workflow from topic selection, proposal and midterm review to thesis evaluation, defense arrangement and grade publication.

Analytics & Master Data

Academic early warnings, teaching evaluations and teaching-quality analytics; centralized management of students, majors, colleges, grade levels and classes, with batch import.

Four Roles, One Platform

  • Students

    Enroll in and drop courses, view timetables and exam schedules, check grades and request reviews, select graduation project topics and submit theses, and register for internships, competitions and social practice.

  • Faculty

    View teaching assignments, enter grades, advise graduation projects and review proposals, midterms and theses, keep guidance logs, and see teaching evaluation results.

  • Departments

    Review graduation project topics, allocate students, arrange defenses and publish results, manage internships, and monitor progress with department dashboards.

  • Academic Affairs

    Manage curricula and scheduling, run enrollment campaigns and exam arrangements, monitor academic warnings and teaching evaluations, maintain master data with batch import, and oversee graduation projects on global dashboards.
